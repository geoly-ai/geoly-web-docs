To begin optimizing your store's data, you must first complete the installation and initial data synchronization. There are three main phases to getting started: Installation, Category Audit, and Description Audit.

---

## Phase 1: Installation and Initialization

The initial setup ensures that the AI has access to your latest product information.

1.  **Access the App Store**: Log in to your Shopify admin, navigate to the "Apps" menu, and select "Shopify App Store".
2.  **Search for the App**: Enter "GEOly: Catalog Optimization" in the search bar.
3.  **Install and Authorize**: Click the "Install" button and confirm the necessary permissions to complete the authorization process.
4.  **Sync Data**: Once installed, enter the app Dashboard. Under the "Optimize your store in 3 steps" section, click the **Sync Data** button.
    * *Note:* This step fetches the latest product data from your Shopify database to ensure consistency.
5.  **Wait for Completion**: Monitor the progress bar until it finishes; the Dashboard will then display your current synchronization status.

## Phase 2: Product Category Audit Setup 

This section allows you to automatically map your products to international standard taxonomies.

1.  **Enter Audit Page**: Select the audit option from the left-hand menu.
2.  **Select Products**: Click the "Select Products" tab and choose the items you wish to optimize from the list.
3.  **Submit Tasks**: Click the "Audit Selected" button at the top or bottom of the page.
4.  **AI Analysis**: A pop-up will indicate that the jobs are queued (e.g., "5 audit jobs queued").
    * The status will change to **PENDING** as the AI provides suggested categories and confidence levels.
5.  **Apply Optimization**: Review and select the products with suggestions, then click the **Accept** button.
6.  **Verification**: Once the status changes to **SYNCED**, the Dashboard progress bar will reflect the completion.

## Phase 3: Product Description Audit Setup

Transform your existing product description into machine-readable, structured HTML lists.

1.  **Access Description Menu**: Click on "Product Description Audit" in the menu.
2.  **Select and Submit**: Check the target products and submit the audit task.
3.  **Review Results**: The AI generates a structured description (including tags like Material, Dimensions, and Weight).
4.  **Accept Audit**: Click **Accept** for the products you have verified.

## Verify Data in Shopify 

1.  Return to your Shopify Products page.
2.  Open an optimized product and scroll to the bottom to find **Product Metafields**.
3.  Expand the section to confirm the AI-generated structure has been successfully written.

> **Tip:** Standardized classification significantly improves ad matching rates for channels like Google Shopping and Facebook.
