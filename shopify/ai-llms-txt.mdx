## I. Initial Installation & Setup

* **Step 1**: Search for **"GEOly: AI LLMs.txt Master"** in the Shopify App Store and complete the installation and authorization process.
* **Sync Data**: Go to the app's main Dashboard and click the **Sync URLs** button within the "Setup Guide" area to fetch the latest product data links from your Shopify database.
* **Configure & Publish**:
    * Navigate to the **Settings** page to refine your store description and select the fields you want AI to access, such as Price and URL.
    * Toggle the status from Unpublished to **Published** to make your `llms.txt` file live and accessible to AI crawlers.

## II. Monitoring & Analyzing Traffic

* **Track AI Visitors**: Use the **Analytics Dashboard** to compare trends between Total Visits and AI Visits (identified AI bots).
* **Granular Queries**: Access the **Recent Access Logs** to identify specifically which bot visited which path (URL) and at what time.

![Dashboard showing publication status and AI traffic data](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/9347/7568/files/20260130-162805.gif?v=1769761737 "Dashboard showing publication status and AI traffic data")

---

## III. Managing Link Content

* **Granular Link Management**:
    * On the **URL Manager** page, you can perform batch operations by selecting multiple target links.
    * Click **Include** to add links to your `llms.txt` file or **Exclude** to hide them from AI bots
    * When the Status shows a green **Included**, the content is officially ready for AI reading.
* **Smart Markdown Generation**:
    * **Generate By System**: Automatically generates structured Markdown content that follows standard specifications.
    * **Generate By Sidekick**: This option uses Shopify's official AI tool to polish and generate your content via Sidekick Skills.
