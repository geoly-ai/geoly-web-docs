---
title: "Members and client access"
description: "Who on your agency team can do what, how project roles work, and how to give a client access to their own project only."
---

Seats are unlimited on every GEOly plan, so you can bring in your whole team and your clients without paying more. What each person can see and do depends on their organization role, and for members, on the projects they've been added to.

## Organization roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control: billing, members, API keys, and every project. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove members and manage every project, but can't change organization roles. |
| **Member** | Only sees the projects they were added to, as a **Project admin** or a **Viewer**. |

On an agency plan, these actions are for owners and admins only:

- buying, upgrading, and canceling the plan (the billing page is only shown to them);
- setting max prompts and platforms for projects;
- starting and converting pitch projects;
- pausing, resuming, restoring, setting expiry dates, and deleting projects.

If the owner transfers ownership to someone else, the former owner becomes an admin.

## Project roles

Owners and admins can see every project. Everyone else only sees the projects they were added to, with one of two roles:

| Project role | What they can do in that project |
| --- | --- |
| **Project admin** | Change the project's data, settings, and integrations, and manage its members. |
| **Viewer** | View the project's data and chat with the Agent, but can't change anything. |

## Outside collaborators

An outside collaborator isn't a member of your organization. They:

- can only open the projects they were added to;
- can't see your member list or organization settings;
- don't count as organization members;
- don't get skills shared with the whole organization, and the Agent doesn't use your company context for them;
- leave the organization when they're removed from their last project.

On **Settings → Organization → Members**, you can turn a member into an outside collaborator with **Convert to outside collaborator**, or the other way round with **Convert to organization member**. Their projects and project roles stay the same.

## Give a client access to their project

Add the client as an outside collaborator on their own project, with the **Viewer** role. They see their project and nothing else from your organization. Choose **Project admin** instead if the client should be able to change the project.

**From the client's project:**

1. Switch to the client's project and open **Settings → Project → Members**.
2. Click **Add people** and enter the client's email addresses.
3. Under **Role**, choose **Viewer**.
4. Leave **Organization member** off. It's off by default, and while it's off they join only this project, as an outside collaborator.
5. Click **Invite** with the number of people, for example **Invite 1**.

**From the organization's member list:**

1. Open **Settings → Organization → Members** and click **Invite**.
2. Enter the client's email addresses.
3. Under **Access**, choose the client's project, not **Organization admin (all projects)**.
4. Under **Role**, choose **Viewer**.
5. Leave **Organization member** off, then click **Invite** with the number of people, for example **Invite 1**.

The client gets an invitation email and joins once they accept. They need a GEOly account to sign in.

<Tip>
Add your own team as organization members. Account managers who should only work on some clients can be members with the **Project admin** role on those projects. Give organization admin only to the people who manage billing, credits, and projects.
</Tip>
