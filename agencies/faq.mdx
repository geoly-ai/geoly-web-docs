### What is a tracking credit?

1 tracking credit is 1 prompt on 1 platform for 1 day, and a month always counts as 30 days. A project with 20 prompts on 3 platforms uses 20 × 3 × 30 = 1,800 credits a month. See [Tracking credits](/agencies/tracking-credits).

### How many prompts does my plan cover?

On 3 platforms every day, Agency Starter's 27,000 credits cover about 300 prompts, and Agency Scale's 85,000 cover about 944. On all 4 platforms, that's 225 and 708.

### Do credits a project doesn't use go back to the pool?

No. Credits count what you allocate, not what runs. A project holds its full allocation until you lower its max prompts or platforms, pause it, let it expire, or delete it.

### Is there a minimum per project?

Yes, 900 credits a month. For example 10 prompts on 3 platforms, or 30 prompts on 1 platform.

### Can each client track different platforms?

Yes. Every client project has its own platforms, chosen from ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. The project's credits change with the number of platforms. There's no waiting period between changes.

### What if I run out of credits?

Lower another project's max prompts or platforms, or pause a project you're not working on. On Agency Starter, you can also upgrade to Agency Scale with **Upgrade plan** on **Settings → Projects**. On Agency Scale, click **Contact us to raise the pool** there instead. See [When the pool runs short](/agencies/tracking-credits#when-the-pool-runs-short).

### Do pitch projects use credits?

No. A pitch project runs free for 7 days with up to 40 prompts. It doesn't count toward the pool or your client project limit. You can run 3 at a time and start up to 6 a month. See [Pitch projects](/agencies/pitch-projects).

### Does an expired or paused project still count?

It no longer counts toward the pool, but it still takes up a client project slot. To free the slot, delete the project. See [Manage client projects](/agencies/manage-projects).

### Can my clients log in to see their data?

Yes. Invite them as outside collaborators on their own project with the **Viewer** role. They need a GEOly account, and seats are unlimited. See [Members and client access](/agencies/members-and-access).

### Can I pay yearly or try an agency plan first?

Agency plans are billed monthly and have no free trial. To show a prospect real data, use a pitch project.

### Can I downgrade, or switch back to a brand plan?

Neither is self-serve. Contact us and we'll help. See [Buy and manage your plan](/agencies/billing).

### Do agency plans include the Agent and GEOly MCP?

Yes. Agency Starter includes 45,000 AI Credits a month for the [Agent](/agent/overview) and other AI features, and Agency Scale includes 150,000. Queries about public industry data through [GEOly MCP](/developers/mcp/introduction), the CLI, or the API use industry data credits: 75,000 a month on Agency Starter and 200,000 on Agency Scale.
