GEOly is a GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) data platform. It measures how AI engines — ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini and Google AI Overviews — mention, rank, cite and recommend brands, scores that visibility with AIGVR and Share of Model, and extends the same measurement to the AI shopping shelf with Share of Card. It also publishes an industry-level dataset so any brand or category can be looked up without setup.
